all of life is just lived through your own senses and memory... you can't just experience what it's like from someone else's experience, or even your own lived experience again. we tell each other stories, which can only tell part of the truth, but true communication is not possible. storytellers have always been valued in any society, they have held status based on their performances, but oftentimes they are just weavers of lies. there is basically no difference between a cult leader, and a Buddha, or a Muhammad, or a Jesus.

there are no relationships we have with others, we actually have relationships with mental models of others inside of our own heads. psychopaths are just more effective at manipulating these models, they are better equipped to manipulate reality in this way, at some social cost. neurotypical people have great difficulty letting go, compartmentalizing, there is more cognitive dissonance when they lie to themselves and others.

all of this is to say, those people I have missed so dearly, or who I have loved or hated, only ever existed in relation to how they made me feel. sometimes the distinction between those I know personally, and those I don't but who have made me feel something, gets blurry and parasocial. this is often how I feel at a concert having some sort of a shared experience with the artist. I'd like to think I am in tune with my emotions, but as a survival mechanism I can't reveal how I feel, it's necessary to lie about how I feel as a man. I have felt the deepest connections fall apart, and then I think that it was all an illusion... perhaps what we think of as a connection is really just a shared delusion and I am just rediscovering solipsism from first principles???
